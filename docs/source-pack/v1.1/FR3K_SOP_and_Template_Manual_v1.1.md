# FR3K Standard Operating Procedure & Template Manual

**Version:** 1.1  
**Date:** August 2026  
**Identity:** Free Communications. Open Infrastructure. AI Driven. Community Built.

## Operating Position

FR3K is experimental, supplementary community infrastructure. It must not be represented as a replacement for official warnings, emergency services, licensed public-safety systems, commercial telecommunications, or professional engineering advice. Field installation, radio operation, electrical systems, drones, public-land access and emergency exercises require the permissions, competent review and legal controls applicable to the specific activity.

### Non-negotiable gates

1. No unresolved P0 safety, legal, permission, privacy or security issue.
2. No permanent or public-site deployment without written authority and a maintenance/removal owner.
3. No resilience, range, uptime or AI performance claim without reproducible evidence.
4. No autonomous AI publication; a named human approves every public asset.
5. No conceptual or AI-generated image presented as proof of a real deployment.

## Roles

| Role | Accountable for |
|---|---|
| Program Lead | mission, priorities, partnerships, final go/no-go decisions |
| Engineering Lead | system design, test evidence, technical sign-off |
| RF Lead | radio configuration, ACMA compliance record, interference controls |
| Electrical Lead | solar, battery, protection and unattended power review |
| Operations / Field Lead | permissions, site access, safety and deployment records |
| Content Lead / Producer | briefs, scripts, production calendar and publication quality |
| Community Lead | onboarding, moderation, events and participation pathways |
| Governance / Security Lead | privacy, incidents, conflicts, access and risk controls |
| Maintainer | releases, documentation, issue triage and contribution quality |

## Universal controls

- Name one accountable owner and one reviewer before work begins.
- Link the work item to its brief, decision record, source pack and evidence folder.
- Stop work when a P0 safety, permission, legal, privacy or security issue is unresolved.
- Record assumptions separately from measured facts; never publish an assumption as a result.
- Store outputs using the approved naming convention and update the control workbook.

## Naming and Archive Standard

`FR3K_[asset-type]_[work-item-or-node]_[YYYYMMDD]_[version].[ext]`

Every archive entry must retain the raw source, final asset, licence/provenance, owner, reviewer, version, publication URL and related decision/evidence records. Public exports must be generated from a reviewed source rather than becoming the only copy.

## Review Cadence

- Daily: field safety, incidents, moderation and publishing queue.
- Weekly: 90-day plan, content evidence, network health and blocked tasks.
- Monthly: KPIs, finance, risks, volunteer health and source freshness.
- Quarterly: architecture, privacy, permissions, funding mix and strategic priorities.
- Annually: full SOP, brand, insurance, legal and governance review.
