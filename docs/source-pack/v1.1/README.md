## Governing assumptions

- FR3K is **experimental and supplementary**, not a replacement for official emergency warnings, emergency services or carrier networks.
- Public-land, protected-area, tower, ferry and third-party installations are **permission-gated candidates**, not committed sites.
- Australian radio compliance is assessed for the exact radio mode, bandwidth, power, antenna, installation and current regulatory instrument.
- Reticulum/LXMF and Meshtastic are complementary systems. Transparent bridging remains a research item rather than a launch dependency.
- Range, uptime, cyclone resilience, AI performance and revenue are measured or scenario-labelled—not guaranteed.
- AI-generated media never substitutes for deployment evidence or human technical review.
